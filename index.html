Featured Achievement

IIT BOMBAY DEVSOC OFFICIAL

Designed the official merchandise for DebSoc, IIT Bombay — one of India's most prestigious debate societies.

Selected to design the official merchandise collection for the Debate Society - WE SPEAK, IIT Bombay. The project demanded a visual language that felt simple and minimalist — balancing the society's roots with a bold, modern streetwear aesthetic.

The work included apparel graphics, mockups, and auxiliary print collateral.
