IIT BOMBAY DEBSOC OFFICIAL

Designed the official merchandise for DebSoc, IIT Bombay — one of India's most prestigious debate societies.

Selected to design the official merchandise collection for the Debate Society - WE SPEAK, IIT Bombay. The project demanded a visual language that felt simple and minimalist — balancing the society's roots with a bold, modern streetwear aesthetic.

The work included apparel graphics, mockups, and auxiliary print collateral.

// How I Work

MY PROCESS — how great design happens

01
Discover

Research the brand, audience, and visual landscape. I dig into what makes a project unique before anything gets designed.

02
Concept

Mood boards, references, 2–3 distinct directions. You choose a path — I push it further than you expected.

03
Execute

Pixel-perfect design with obsessive attention to light, shadow, type, and composition. Every detail earns its place.

04
Deliver

Clean files, all formats, no fuss. Assets ready for print, web, or wherever the world needs to see them.
